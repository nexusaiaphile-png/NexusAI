DESIGNED FOR MOBILE ACCESS

Learning should not depend on owning a laptop.

Lessons are planned around smartphone-friendly materials, WhatsApp communication, short videos and voice notes, with online sessions and alternative learning materials where needed. The programme intends to provide learner data support and seek sponsored or loan devices for participants who need them, subject to sponsorship and availability.

✓ Mobile-friendly lessons and materials
✓ Practical assignments and feedback
✓ Online tutor-led learning, subject to appointment
✓ NexusAI certificate of completion for eligible completers
APPLICATIONS & SELECTION

Interested in taking part?

The proposed first intake is 10 participants aged 18–30 from Bergville and surrounding communities. Applicants will be assessed through a formal application process. There is no application fee.

Applicants will be asked to complete a printed application form by hand, sign it, scan all pages into one PDF and email it to us. Applications are not accepted by WhatsApp or hand delivery.

Download the application form ↓Download the PDF, complete it by hand and sign it.Scan all pages into ONE PDF and email it to nexusai.aphile@gmail.com.Applications are not accepted through WhatsApp or by hand delivery. Questions? Call or WhatsApp 068 265 5600.

Certificates are issued by NexusAI for programme completion. This programme is not presented as a SAQA-accredited qualification or formal accredited course.
