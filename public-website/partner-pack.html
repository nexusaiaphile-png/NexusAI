About the opportunity

NexusAI Technologies is developing Retail Intelligence technology intended to correlate agreed point-of-sale (POS) transaction exception data with relevant camera events and footage through authorised integrations. We are building relationships with organisations that understand retail operations, security infrastructure and technology delivery.

Who can express interest?

CCTV installers

Businesses installing or maintaining CCTV systems and supporting retail customers.

Security companies

Guarding and protection providers with relevant retail relationships.

Distributors and resellers

Technology channel businesses interested in connecting with installer networks.

System integrators and retailers

Organisations with POS, CCTV, cloud integration or retail operational expertise.

How the partner process works

  1. Create a partner account through the Partner Portal using your company details and a password.
  2. Your registration is marked pending while NexusAI reviews the organisation and proposed collaboration.
  3. Once approved, sign in with your email and password and complete any required partner agreement or onboarding steps.
  4. Partners introduce companies that have expressly authorised the engagement, or approved activation partners use their own restricted account once that workflow is available.
  5. Qualifying activations are checked, attributed and recorded before commission is approved.

Proposed commission

Approved partners may earn R100 for each qualifying camera activation successfully verified and attributed to their partner profile. The commission is subject to a signed agreement. The agreement will define eligibility, attribution, verification, payment timing, cancellations or reversals, tax responsibilities and dispute handling. No commission is earned merely by submitting a lead or registering interest.

Partner levels under consideration

  • Registered Partner: introduces authorised companies and qualified opportunities.
  • Certified Activation Partner: completes onboarding and may receive individual, permission-limited activation access when available.
  • Strategic Business Partner: explores broader regional, national or channel collaboration under a separate written scope.
Important security and product disclosures
No shared master administrator key will be issued. Any future activation access will use individual accounts with restricted permissions. Partners may only access or activate systems with the relevant business's express authorisation. NexusAI is preparing for its first controlled real-world retail pilot; the solution has not yet been validated in a live retail deployment. Compatibility, performance and commercial availability remain subject to testing. No claims are made that the product prevents theft or identifies wrongdoing.
