03 — THE TECHNOLOGY DIRECTION

Connect transaction context with camera evidence.

NexusAI Retail Intelligence is being developed to correlate POS transaction exceptions with relevant camera events and footage through authorised integrations.

POS information

Receive agreed transaction records or exception data from a compatible point-of-sale system.

Camera events

Explore authorised access to relevant Hikvision camera events through Hik-Partner Pro.

Correlation

Test whether transaction timestamps, till mappings and camera events can be matched reliably.

Review

Present relevant footage or snapshots for authorised store personnel to review.

Development status: NexusAI is preparing for its first controlled real-world retail pilot. The solution has not yet been validated in a live retail deployment. Compatibility, performance and commercial availability must be established through testing. We do not claim to prevent theft, identify wrongdoing or replace existing security personnel.

06 — COMMON QUESTIONS

Before you register.

A few important points about the current stage of the programme.

Is NexusAI already deployed in stores?

No. NexusAI is preparing for its first controlled real-world pilot. We are not representing the product as commercially proven or live in retail stores.

Does a registration guarantee a partnership?

No. Registration is an expression of interest. Each organisation will be assessed for fit, capability and potential mutual value.

Must our company use Hikvision?

Hikvision and Hik-Partner Pro experience is relevant to the current integration direction, but we welcome discussions with other qualified retail technology and security organisations too.

Are there partnership fees?

No fee or commercial terms are being requested through this expression-of-interest form. Any future arrangement would require separate discussion and a written agreement.

What happens after we submit?

NexusAI can review the information you choose to send and contact you to arrange an introductory discussion where there is a suitable opportunity.

How does the R100 camera commission work?

The proposed commission is R100 for each qualifying camera activation that is successfully verified and attributed to an approved partner. The written partner agreement will explain eligibility and payment terms.

Will partners receive an admin key?

No shared master admin key will be issued. Approved activation partners may receive their own secure, permission-limited portal access when that workflow is available. Partners may only activate cameras for businesses that have authorised the work.
