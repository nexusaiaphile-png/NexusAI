## 2. Appointment and scope

NexusAI appoints the Distributor on a non-exclusive, independent-contractor basis to identify and introduce suitable CCTV installers, security-technology companies, distributors and system integrators to NexusAI's proposed Retail Intelligence opportunity.

The Distributor may not bind NexusAI, quote or vary NexusAI pricing, make product-performance guarantees, represent that a pilot has succeeded, or claim Hikvision endorsement or approval of NexusAI's Retail Intelligence product.

NexusAI's Retail Intelligence solution is under development and is intended to correlate point-of-sale transaction data with supported Hik-Partner Pro camera events. The first controlled real-world retail pilot has not yet been completed as at the date of this draft.

## 3. Referral process and attribution

3.1 The Distributor may submit a referral only after obtaining the relevant business contact's permission to be introduced to NexusAI.

3.2 Each referral must identify the installer or integrator business and the authorised contact person. The Distributor must not upload bulk contact lists, confidential customer information, or personal information without a lawful basis and the required permissions.

3.3 NexusAI will review each referral and may accept, reject or request clarification. A submission, introduction, meeting, quotation or expression of interest alone does not qualify for a reward.

3.4 Attribution disputes, duplicate referrals and pre-existing NexusAI relationships will be assessed using documented referral history and the terms agreed by the parties.

## 5. No authority, employment or exclusivity

The Distributor is an independent contractor. This agreement does not create employment, partnership, joint venture, agency, franchise or exclusivity. The Distributor bears its own operating costs unless separately agreed in writing.

## 6. Confidentiality and intellectual property

Each party must protect the other party's non-public business, technical, customer and commercial information and use it only for this agreement. NexusAI retains all rights in its name, software, platform, documentation and intellectual property. No licence is granted except the limited permission to use approved materials for agreed referral activity.

## 7. Privacy and data protection

Each party must comply with applicable privacy and data-protection laws, including the Protection of Personal Information Act 4 of 2013 (POPIA), where applicable. The Distributor must obtain permission before submitting an individual's business contact details and must provide accurate information about the intended introduction. The parties must promptly notify one another of suspected unauthorised access, loss or disclosure of referral information.

NexusAI will publish an appropriate privacy notice describing its processing purposes, lawful basis, retention periods, data-subject rights and contact details before the portal is opened for public referrals.
