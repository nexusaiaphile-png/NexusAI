Retailer assessment privacy information

NexusAI Technologies uses information submitted through this form to evaluate whether a retailer's POS and authorised camera environment may be technically compatible with NexusAI Retail Intelligence, to respond to the application and to administer any assessment or pilot the parties separately agree to.

Information submitted

This may include business and professional contact details, retail environment details, POS and CCTV platform information, objectives, notes and the technical PDF uploaded by the applicant.

Documents and sensitive information

Do not upload passwords, API keys, access tokens, payment-card information, customer transaction exports, CCTV footage or unnecessary personal information. Upload only technical documents you are authorised to share. Access to submitted files is restricted to authorised NexusAI administrators for assessment purposes.

Retention and requests

Submitted information is retained only as needed to assess and manage the enquiry, meet applicable legal obligations and protect the platform. To request access, correction or deletion, or to raise a privacy concern, contact NexusAI through the contact details published on getnexusai.co.za.

Submitting this form is not marketing consent, does not guarantee compatibility and does not start a pilot. Any pilot requires technical readiness, appropriate authorisations and a separate written agreement.
